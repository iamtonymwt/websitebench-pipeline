    About Drills

    Power drills are key power tool staples for every DIYer and homeowner. Power drills can help perform a variety of tasks around your home. Most commonly, they are used for drilling holes and driving screws. While there are many different types of drills, its good to know which one will be best for your projects.

    Corded drills can be a great option for projects when you have an electrical outlet handy. While cordless drills offer unlimited range and maximum power with a fully charged battery. For projects that require maximum torque, compact drills or brushless drills are best your best bet.

    Specialty drills include SDS hammer drills, impact drivers and SDS max hammer drills. These types of drills are primarily used for drilling into concrete, stone and masonry. Electric screwdrivers also speed up the process of driving screws into various materials around your home.

    If you are looking to drive screws, a screw gun is the fastest way to get the job done. For projects that require larger fastening quantities, an impact driver that uses specialty bits will help you get projects that require longer screws and lag bolts done.

    To ensure your drill delivers the ultimate performance, pair it with the right drill bit for the material you are using. Wood, metal, plastic and masonry each require different types of drill bits. If you use the wrong drill bit, your drill will have to work harder and may provide subpar results.

    If you are looking for more than one type drill for your workbench, consider investing in a combo kit that offers a standard power drill, a specialty drill, a charger, batteries and carrying case.

    No matter what type of project you need help with, The Home Depot is the source for your fastening and power drill needs.
